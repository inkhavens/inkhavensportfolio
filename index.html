About me

I am a Roblox programmer from the United States and I am 18 years old.

I joined Roblox in 2016. Back then I wanted to be a builder. I spent a long time on it and it never clicked. I was not good at it and I knew that, so in 2019 I went looking for another path and found programming instead. That one stuck, and I have been doing it ever since.

Since then I have shipped several games that found real players. Every game on this page is one I own or help develop. I do not count past commission work here, even though that work added a large number of visits that you will not see in my totals. The numbers on this page are only from my own projects.

Along the way I have become someone YouTubers hand their projects to. Creators with real audiences bring me in to program the games they are about to put in front of millions of viewers. That only happens if people trust your work to hold up under pressure, and I have earned that trust more than once.

None of it came fast. I have put a very large amount of time and effort into this work, and I am still going.
